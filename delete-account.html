Delete your Halal Games Studio account

Last updated: 2026-05-08

The fastest way: delete it inside the app

Open Halal Games Studio → Settings tab → scroll to Account → tap Delete account. Your account and all associated data are removed immediately.

Don't have the app installed?

Email us using the link below. Include your username (the @handle others use to find you), and we will permanently remove your account within 30 days.

Request account deletion by email

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (display name and @username)
  • Your friend list and all friend requests sent or received
  • Your saved game scores and round history
  • Your authentication record (the anonymous Supabase auth row tied to your device)

What is retained

  • Subscription / purchase records — for tax and accounting reasons we may retain anonymised purchase records as required by law. Active subscriptions are not cancelled by account deletion; cancel a Pro subscription separately in your App Store or Google Play account before deleting.
  • App-side caches on your device — clear the app's local data via your phone's Settings if you want to remove cached identifiers from the device itself. The app stores no personal data on your device beyond a language preference and your auth session token.

How long does it take?

In-app deletion is immediate. Email-based deletion is processed within 30 days; we will reply confirming completion. Backup tapes that contain a snapshot of the database are overwritten on a 30-day rolling basis.

Questions

Email halalgamesstudio.support@gmail.com for anything not covered here. Our Privacy Policy describes what data we collect and how it is used.
