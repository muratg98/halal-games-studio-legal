Halal Games

Five halal party games for Muslim families and friends. Pass the phone, race the letter, guess the word. No music, no ads, no tracking.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

The games

  • Stop the Bus — letter race across Islamic categories. Online multiplayer.
  • Word Imposter — spot the one who doesn't know. Pass-and-play, 3-10 players.
  • Heads Up — phone on forehead, friends describe the word. Single phone.
  • My Other Side — colour-coded conversation cards for two.
  • Ice Breaker — halal first-meeting prompts for marriage-age users.

What's different

  • Genuinely halal content. No music, no dance, no inappropriate themes.
  • No ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking. Period.
  • Anonymous accounts by default — pick a @username, that's all anyone sees.
  • Friends-only leaderboards. No rage-bait, no global rankings.
