Privacy Policy

Halal Games Studio · Effective 26 April 2026 · Last updated 26 April 2026

This policy explains what information Halal Games Studio (the "app", "we", "us") collects, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it. The app is operated from the United Kingdom and is offered worldwide. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Who we are

Halal Games Studio is an independently-developed mobile game. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights below, contact halalgamesstudio.support@gmail.com.

The data we collect

We deliberately collect as little as possible. We do not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone, or camera.

WhatWhyWhere it lives
Anonymous account ID (a random UUID created when you first open the app) To remember your friends list, scores, and subscription status across sessions and devices Your device, plus Supabase (our database provider)
Username and display name (chosen by you) So friends can find you and see who scored what Supabase

Crash reports and basic diagnostics (provided by Apple / Google to developers, not collected by us directly) To investigate and fix bugs Apple / Google developer dashboards

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, social-media tracking pixels, or any third-party advertising network. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

How we use your data

  • To run the game features you ask for (multiplayer lobbies, friends, leaderboards, subscriptions).
  • To support your account if you contact us.
  • To meet legal obligations (tax records on subscription purchases, for example).

We do not use your data to profile you, target you with advertising, or train machine-learning models.

Lawful bases (UK GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — to deliver the app, run subscriptions, and run multiplayer features you've signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the app running securely and to investigate abuse.
  • Legal obligation — to keep tax-relevant subscription records.
  • Consent — for anything not covered above; you can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

Third parties we use

The app sends data to a small number of processors so it can function:

  • Supabase (database, anonymous authentication, realtime). Hosts profiles, friendships, scores. supabase.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat (subscription state). Receives the anonymous user ID and your purchase status from Apple / Google. revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play (purchases). When you subscribe, the actual transaction is between you and Apple or Google, governed by their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Expo / Apple / Google SDKs — operating-system-provided components for fonts, motion sensors, screen orientation, system sharing, and haptics. These run on-device and do not send data back to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. The "Ice Breaker" game in particular is intended for adults of marriageable age and contains conversation prompts appropriate to that context. If you are a parent who believes a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete your account and the data associated with it.
  • Object to processing or restrict it.
  • Receive a portable copy of your data.
  • Complain to a supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk).

To exercise any of these, email halalgamesstudio.support@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. To delete your account in-app, go to Settings → Sign out & start fresh, or email us.

Data retention

  • Profile, friendship, and score data are kept while your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, we delete or anonymise this data within 30 days.
  • Subscription receipts and tax records are kept for at least 6 years where law requires.

Security

Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Data at rest in Supabase is encrypted at the storage layer and access is restricted by row-level security policies that limit each user to their own data and accepted friends' data only. We cannot guarantee absolute security; if we discover a personal-data breach we will notify affected users and the relevant supervisory authority where the law requires.

International transfers

Our processors (Supabase, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) may store and process data in countries outside the UK / EEA, including the United States. Where this happens we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards offered by those providers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, notify you in-app. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
